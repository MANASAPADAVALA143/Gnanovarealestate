# Gnanova Real Estate — Demo Script (10 min)

## Before you start
- Have all 3 servers running (see demo-runbook.md)
- Have test lead "Ahmed Al Mansouri" already in the system
- Have your phone ready for live WhatsApp demo
- Open these tabs in advance:
  1. http://localhost:3000 (landing page)
  2. http://localhost:3000/dashboard (home)
  3. http://localhost:3000/dashboard/leads
  4. http://localhost:3000/dashboard/pipeline
  5. http://localhost:3000/dashboard/tasks
  6. http://localhost:3000/dashboard/analytics

---

## Minute 1 — Open with the pain point
(Show landing page -> scroll to ROI calculator)

SAY:
"Quick question before I show you anything -
when a lead comes in from Property Finder or Bayut
at 11pm, what happens to it?"

[Let them answer - usually: "agent calls next morning"
or "it goes into a spreadsheet"]

SAY:
"That's the gap we close. Studies show that if you
don't respond to a lead within 5 minutes, you lose
80% of them. Most Dubai brokerages respond in 4-6 hours.
We bring that down to under 2 minutes - automatically,
on WhatsApp, even at 11pm."

[Show ROI calculator - plug in their number of leads/month]

---

## Minute 2 — Show the manager view
(Switch to /dashboard home)

SAY:
"This is what your sales manager sees every morning.

SAY:

"Every lead has a full history in one place.

"Your agents don't have to log anything.

(Switch to /dashboard/pipeline)

on this board - New, Contacted, Qualified,

## Objection handling

### "We already have a CRM"
SAY:
"Which one? Most CRMs here are contact books -
they don't auto-reply on WhatsApp,
don't auto-assign leads,
don't auto-close tasks when a lead responds.
What does yours do automatically?"

### "We need to think about it"
SAY:
"Completely fine.
Can I ask - is it the price, the setup effort,
or something you saw in the demo?
I ask because if it's setup,
that's on us - we handle the whole onboarding."

### "We don't have budget right now"
SAY:
"I understand.
The pilot is 7 days free - no commitment.
If it doesn't recover at least one extra deal
in 30 days, cancel with one message.
We carry the risk, not you."

### "We want more features first"
SAY:
"What's the one thing you wish your current
system did that it doesn't?
If it's on our roadmap I'll tell you when -
if it's critical I'll see if we can
prioritize it for your account."
